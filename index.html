Hello, traveler! Welcome to my silly little cavern... or whatever

I'm Silly! You probably know that, though. This website is for the fonts and themes I have made for people like YOU! Maybe you'd like to take a look?

These fonts should work on most Discord clients that run off of Vendetta, or Kettu, or whatever, someone pls correct me on this. You may have to reload the page to check for any updates. To add fonts to it, go to Settings, then click Fonts. After that, click the + icon to add a font. Then tap Import font entries from a link. From there, paste the font link in, and click Import. Scroll down, then click Import again. There you go. You have just installed a brand new font!

Side note, if you want to get a font from somewhere else, I recommend heading to https://bunny-google-fonts.vercel.app/

The silly cat you likely noticed is Rose! She was made by Gregory Bell, in 2004. You can find all sorts of nekos at WebNeko! Give them some love!!
